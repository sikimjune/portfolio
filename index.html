About_Me

Ciao, I’m

Kim June A. Moroscallo

Misamis Occidental, Northern Mindanao, Philippines

I’m a UI/UX Designer and Computer Engineering student who enjoys turning ideas into digital experiences that are simple, engaging, and easy to use. I’m interested in the balance between creativity and functionality, from designing interfaces and prototypes to figuring out how small details can make a big difference in how people interact with a product.

Outside of design, I’m constantly learning, experimenting, and building things that challenge me to think differently. I enjoy exploring new tools, improving my skills, and bringing ideas to life through both design and code. For me, good design is not just about making something look good—it’s about making it feel right to the people using it.

I’m currently studying at University of Science and Technology of Southern Philippines as a Second-year Computer Engineering.
